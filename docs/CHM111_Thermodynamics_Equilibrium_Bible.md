## Module 1: Chemical Thermodynamics & Spontaneity
* **First Law:** $\Delta U = q + w$ ($w = -P\Delta V$ for expansion work).
* **Enthalpy:** $\Delta H = \Delta U + \Delta(PV) = \Delta U + \Delta n_g RT$.
* **Gibbs Free Energy & Spontaneity:**
  $$\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$$
  * $\Delta G < 0$: Spontaneous reaction
  * $\Delta G = 0$: Dynamic equilibrium ($T_{eq} = \frac{\Delta H}{\Delta S}$)
  * $\Delta G > 0$: Non-spontaneous reaction

## Module 2: Chemical Equilibrium & Le Chatelier
* **Equilibrium Constant Relation:**
  $$\Delta G^\circ = -RT\ln K$$
  $$K_p = K_c (RT)^{\Delta n_g}$$
* **Le Chatelier's Shifts:**
  * Increase $[Reactant]$ $\to$ Shift RIGHT.
  * Increase $[Product]$ $\to$ Shift LEFT.
  * Increase Total Pressure $\to$ Shift to side with FEWER gas moles.
  * Exothermic ($\Delta H < 0$): Increase $T$ $\to$ Shift LEFT ($K$ decreases).
  * Endothermic ($\Delta H > 0$): Increase $T$ $\to$ Shift RIGHT ($K$ increases).
  * Catalyst $\to$ No effect on equilibrium position or $K$ (only accelerates rate).

## Module 3: Chemical Kinetics
* **Arrhenius Equation:**
  $$k = A e^{-E_a / RT} \iff \ln k = \ln A - \frac{E_a}{RT}$$
  $$\ln\left(\frac{k_2}{k_1}\right) = \frac{E_a}{R}\left(\frac{1}{T_1} - \frac{1}{T_2}\right)$$
