## Module 1: Units, Dimensions & Physical Quantities
* **Dimension Table (High-Yield):**
  * Velocity: $[LT^{-1}]$
  * Acceleration: $[LT^{-2}]$
  * Force / Weight: $[MLT^{-2}]$
  * Work / Energy / Torque: $[ML^2T^{-2}]$
  * Power: $[ML^2T^{-3}]$
  * Pressure / Stress / Young's Modulus: $[ML^{-1}T^{-2}]$
  * Momentum / Impulse: $[MLT^{-1}]$
  * Surface Tension: $[MT^{-2}]$
  * Viscosity $(\eta)$: $[ML^{-1}T^{-1}]$
* **Principle of Dimensional Homogeneity:**
  * In any valid physical relation $A = B + C$, all terms must possess identical dimensions.
  * Arguments of trigonometric, exponential, and logarithmic functions are dimensionless ($M^0L^0T^0$).

## Module 2: Vectors & Scalars
* **Resultant of Two Vectors:**
  $$R = \sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + 2AB\cos\theta}$$
  * Maximum at $\theta = 0^\circ$: $R = A + B$
  * Minimum at $\theta = 180^\circ$: $R = |A - B|$
  * Perpendicular at $\theta = 90^\circ$: $R = \sqrt{A^2 + B^2}$
* **Component Form:**
  * $R_x = \sum A_x = \sum A\cos\theta$
  * $R_y = \sum A_y = \sum A\sin\theta$
  * Magnitude: $R = \sqrt{R_x^2 + R_y^2}$
  * Direction: $\alpha = \tan^{-1}(|R_y / R_x|)$ (adjust for quadrant).

## Module 3: Kinematics (Uniformly Accelerated Motion)
1. $v = u + at$
2. $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$
3. $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$
4. $s = \frac{u + v}{2} t$
* **Projectile Motion (Angle $\theta$, speed $u$):**
  * Time of flight: $T = \frac{2u\sin\theta}{g}$
  * Maximum height: $H = \frac{u^2\sin^2\theta}{2g}$
  * Horizontal range: $R = \frac{u^2\sin 2\theta}{g}$ (Maximum at $\theta = 45^\circ$)

## Module 4: Space-Time & Einstein's Relativity
* **First Postulate (Principle of Relativity):** Laws of physics are invariant across all inertial reference frames.
* **Second Postulate (Constancy of Light):** Speed of light $c = 3 \times 10^8\text{ m/s}$ is constant in vacuum for all observers.
* **Relativistic Factors:**
  * Lorentz factor: $\gamma = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}}$
  * Time Dilation: $t = \gamma t_0$ (moving clocks tick slower)
  * Length Contraction: $L = \frac{L_0}{\gamma}$ (moving objects contract in direction of motion)
  * Mass-Energy Equivalence: $E = mc^2$
