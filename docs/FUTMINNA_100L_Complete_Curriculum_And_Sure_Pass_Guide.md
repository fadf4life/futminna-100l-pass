## Part 2: Detailed Syllabus Breakdown for Course Expansion

When expanding this educational pack to cover the remaining 9 courses, use the following topic blueprints:

### 1. PHY 101: General Physics I
- Units, dimensions, and dimensional analysis.
- Vectors (addition, cross/dot products, resolution).
- Kinematics in 1D and 2D (projectiles, circular motion).
- Newton's laws of motion, momentum, impulse, and collisions.
- Work, energy, power, and conservation of mechanical energy.
- Rotational dynamics, moment of inertia, torque.
- Elasticity, Hooke's law, Young's modulus.
- Fluid mechanics (hydrostatic pressure, Archimedes principle, Bernoulli's equation, viscosity).
- Thermal physics: Temperature scales, heat capacity, latent heat, gas laws ($PV=nRT$), first law of thermodynamics.

### 2. CHM 101: General Chemistry I
- Atomic structure, quantum numbers, electron configurations, Hund's rule, Pauli exclusion principle.
- Periodic table trends: ionization energy, electron affinity, electronegativity, atomic/ionic radii.
- Chemical bonding: ionic, covalent, coordinate, hydrogen bonding, hybridization ($sp, sp^2, sp^3$).
- Stoichiometry, mole concept, limiting reactants, empirical/molecular formulas.
- States of matter: ideal gas laws, Dalton's law of partial pressures, Graham's law of diffusion.
- Chemical equilibrium ($K_c, K_p$, Le Chatelier's principle).
- Acids, bases, and salts: pH, buffer solutions, neutralization, solubility product ($K_{sp}$).
- Thermochemistry: enthalpy, Hess's Law, calorimetry.
- Electrochemistry: oxidation numbers, redox balancing, galvanic cells, Nernst equation.

### 3. GST 101: Use of English I
- Reading comprehension strategies for academic texts.
- Sentence structures, clauses, and sentence errors (run-ons, fragments, comma splices).
- Concord and subject-verb agreement rules.
- Lexis and structure (idiomatic expressions, collocations, register).
- Punctuation rules and mechanics.
- Phonetics and phonology (vowel sounds, diphthongs, consonant clusters, primary stress placement).

### 4. CSC 101 / CPT 111: Introduction to Computing
- History and generations of computers.
- Computer hardware, architecture (CPU, ALU, Registers, Cache, Memory hierarchy).
- Number systems: Binary, Octal, Decimal, Hexadecimal conversions and arithmetic.
- System software vs. Application software (OS concepts, compilers vs. interpreters).
- Data communication and networking basics (LAN, WAN, Topologies, Internet protocols).
- Algorithms, flowcharts, and pseudo-code logic.
- Fundamental programming constructs (variables, data types, conditionals, loops).

### 5. MAT 102: Elementary Mathematics II
- Limits and continuity of functions (L'Hôpital's Rule).
- Differentiation from first principles and standard rules (Product, Quotient, Chain rules).
- Implicit differentiation, parametric differentiation, logarithmic differentiation.
- Applications of differentiation: tangents and normals, maximum and minimum, rate of change.
- Integration: standard integrals, integration by substitution, integration by parts, partial fractions.
- Definite integrals and area under curves.
- Coordinate geometry: straight lines, circles, parabola, ellipse, hyperbola.
- Vectors in 3D: dot product, cross product, vector equations of lines and planes.

## Part 3: The "Sure-Pass" Pedagogical Architecture

To make scoring an **A** virtually certain for any student using the platform, the system incorporates 4 active learning loops:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        THE "SURE-PASS" LEARNING ENGINE                 │
└───────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┘
                                    │
           ┌────────────────────────┼────────────────────────┐
           ▼                        ▼                        ▼
    [1] Rapid-Fire          [2] The Mistake          [3] The 20-Second
        Active Recall           Vault Killer             Speed Hacks
        (3D Flashcards)      (Zero-Defect Goal)      (CBT Elimination)
```

### 1. The Mistake Vault (Weakness Elimination)
- In traditional studying, students review what they already know.
- The **Mistake Vault** automatically catches every question a student misses across CBT mock exams and topic drills in browser `localStorage`.
- **The "Zero-Vault Rule":** Students are coached to drill their vault repeatedly until the vault count hits **0**. When a student's mistake vault is empty, exam failure is statistically impossible.

### 2. The 20-Second CBT Speed Hack Philosophy
- FUTMINNA e-exams give students approximately **54 seconds per question**.
- Students who solve questions purely through traditional textbook derivations run out of time on question 35.
- The educational pack provides:
  - **Substitution Hacks:** E.g., setting $n = 1, 2$ in mathematical induction or $x = 1, y = 1$ in binomial coefficients.
  - **Reverse Back-Solving:** Testing options directly into equations.
  - **Sign & Dimension Elimination:** Discarding 2 wrong options in 3 seconds based on sign consistency or matrix dimensions.

### 3. Gamification for Gen-Z Retention
- **Daily Streak Tracker:** Encourages 15 minutes of daily practice instead of stressful overnight cramming.
- **Experience Points (XP):** Rewards problem solving, flashcard flips, and completed mocks with progressive rank badges (*100L Fresher* $\to$ *Distinction Master*).
- **Haptic/Sound Feedback:** Synthesized via Web Audio API (zero audio files needed, works 100% offline).

## Part 4: Deployment Options

### Option A: Progressive Web App (PWA) — Zero Data, Instant Access
- **Offline First:** Cached via Service Worker (`sw.js`). Students can open it in airplane mode in campus hostels (Bosso or Gidan Kwano) without consuming data.
- **Home Screen Installable:** Students simply tap "Install Now" or "Add to Home Screen" on Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS).
- **Instant Updates:** When you add questions for PHY 101 or CHM 101, the Service Worker updates in the background automatically.

### Option B: Expo Native Mobile App (React Native)
- Located in `c:\Research\Fadil\mobile-app`.
- Can be compiled into a standalone **Android APK (`.apk`)** using Expo Application Services (EAS):
  ```bash
  cd mobile-app
  npm install
  npm install -g eas-cli
  eas login
  eas build -p android --profile preview
  ```
- The resulting `.apk` can be shared peer-to-peer via **WhatsApp, Telegram, or Xender**, requiring no app store download for students!
