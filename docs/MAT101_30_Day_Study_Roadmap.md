# 30-Day "Roadmap to an A" Study Plan: FUTMINNA MAT 101
### Guaranteed Grade-A Execution Framework for 100L Students

> **Objective:** Systematically master the entire MAT 101 curriculum, solve all 540 compiled questions, and build reflex-level speed for the Computer-Based Test (CBT).

---

## The 4-Pillar Strategy for MAT 101 CBT Success
1. **Pillar 1 — Concept & Formula Ingestion (30 mins daily):** Review topic theory and flashcards.
2. **Pillar 2 — Targeted Problem Solving (60 mins daily):** Work through 18–20 practice questions from the BSG compiled pack daily.
3. **Pillar 3 — Speed-Hack Drills (20 mins daily):** Practice solving questions using substitution, option elimination, and reverse evaluation under 45 seconds per question.
4. **Pillar 4 — Active Retrieval & Error Logging (15 mins daily):** Record any missed question in your personal "Mistake Vault" and re-solve it before sleep.

## Daily Schedule Breakdown

### WEEK 1: FOUNDATIONS (Sets, Functions & Quadratics)
- **Day 1: Set Theory Fundamentals**
  - Theory: Subsets, power sets, Venn diagrams, set builder notation.
  - Practice: BSG Set Theory Q1 – Q30.
  - Flashcards Deck: Set Theory Laws & De Morgan's.
- **Day 2: Set Theory Mastery & Word Problems**
  - Theory: Three-set Venn diagrams, percentage/population word problems.
  - Practice: BSG Set Theory Q31 – Q60.
  - Speed Drill: 2-set and 3-set overlap formula shortcuts.
- **Day 3: Mapping and Functions Basics**
  - Theory: Domain, codomain, range, injection, surjection, bijection.
  - Practice: BSG Mapping and Functions Q1 – Q30.
  - Flashcards Deck: Function types and invertibility conditions.
- **Day 4: Composite and Inverse Functions**
  - Theory: Function compositions $f \circ g$, finding inverse $f^{-1}(x)$, linear fractional inverse shortcut.
  - Practice: BSG Mapping and Functions Q31 – Q60.
  - Speed Drill: Back-solving $f(x) = y$ instead of finding algebraic inverse.
- **Day 5: Quadratic Equations — Factorization & Formula**
  - Theory: Roots of quadratic equations, discriminant $D = b^2 - 4ac$, nature of roots.
  - Practice: BSG Quadratic Equation Q1 – Q30.
  - Flashcards Deck: Discriminant decision rules.
- **Day 6: Quadratic Equations — Symmetric Roots & Word Problems**
  - Theory: $\alpha + \beta = -b/a$, $\alpha\beta = c/a$, $\alpha^2 + \beta^2$, $\alpha^3 + \beta^3$, forming equations.
  - Practice: BSG Quadratic Equation Q31 – Q60.
  - Speed Drill: Back-solving options into quadratic equations.
- **Day 7: Weekly Diagnostic Mock 1**
  - Mock Test 1: 50 randomized questions covering Sets, Functions, and Quadratics (45-minute timed test).
  - Review: Thorough analysis of all missed questions.

### WEEK 2: ALGEBRAIC TOOLS (Surds, Indices/Logs & Inequalities)
- **Day 8: Surds — Simplification & Operations**
  - Theory: Rules of surds, reducing to simplest form, surd addition/multiplication.
  - Practice: BSG Surd Q1 – Q30.
- **Day 9: Surds — Rationalization & Square Roots of Surds**
  - Theory: Binomial conjugate rationalization, solving $\sqrt{a \pm \sqrt{b}}$.
  - Practice: BSG Surd Q31 – Q60.
  - Speed Drill: Squaring options to match the question expression in 20 seconds.
- **Day 10: Indices & Exponential Equations**
  - Theory: Fractional/negative indices, solving $a^{f(x)} = a^{g(x)}$, simultaneous exponential equations.
  - Practice: BSG Indices and Logarithms Q1 – Q30.
- **Day 11: Logarithms & Base Changes**
  - Theory: Logarithmic properties, change of base formula, logarithmic equations.
  - Practice: BSG Indices and Logarithms Q31 – Q60.
  - Speed Drill: Testing $x=1$ or $x=2$ in logarithmic identities.
- **Day 12: Linear & Quadratic Inequalities**
  - Theory: Sign reversal on negative multiplication, quadratic interval notation, sign charts.
  - Practice: BSG Inequality Q1 – Q30.
- **Day 13: Rational & Absolute Value Inequalities**
  - Theory: $\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)} \ge 0$, $|x-a| < b$ vs $|x-a| > b$, critical test points.
  - Practice: BSG Inequality Q31 – Q60.
  - Speed Drill: Option elimination testing $x=0$ or extreme values.
- **Day 14: Weekly Diagnostic Mock 2**
  - Mock Test 2: 50 randomized questions covering Weeks 1 & 2 topics (45 minutes).
  - Error Analysis: Reviewing calculation slips and sign errors.

### WEEK 3: ADVANCED TOPICS (Complex Numbers, Binomial & Induction)
- **Day 15: Complex Numbers — Arithmetic & Conjugates**
  - Theory: Powers of $i/j$, Cartesian form $x+jy$, addition, multiplication, division by conjugate.
  - Practice: BSG Complex Number Q1 – Q30.
  - Flashcards Deck: Cyclic powers of $i$ and modulus formulas.
- **Day 16: Complex Numbers — Modulus, Argument & Polar Form**
  - Theory: $r = |z|$, $\theta = \text{Arg}(z)$ with quadrant checks, polar form $r(\cos\theta + i\sin\theta)$.
  - Practice: BSG Complex Number Q31 – Q50.
  - Speed Drill: Rapid quadrant identification for angle signs.
- **Day 17: Complex Numbers — De Moivre’s Theorem**
  - Theory: $(r\text{ cis }\theta)^n = r^n\text{ cis}(n\theta)$, roots of unity.
  - Practice: BSG Complex Number Q51 – Q60.
- **Day 18: Binomial Expansion — Standard Terms**
  - Theory: Binomial theorem for positive integers, Pascal's triangle, combination coefficients.
  - Practice: BSG Binomial Expansion Q1 – Q20.
- **Day 19: Binomial Expansion — General Terms & Rational Powers**
  - Theory: $T_{r+1} = \binom{n}{r}a^{n-r}b^r$, term independent of $x$, $(1+x)^{-n}$ expansions.
  - Practice: BSG Binomial Expansion Q21 – Q40.
  - Speed Drill: Setting $x=1$ to evaluate sum of coefficients.
- **Day 20: Mathematical Induction**
  - Theory: 3-step proof protocol (Base $n=1$, Hypothesis $n=k$, Step $n=k+1$), series sum formulas.
  - Practice: BSG Mathematical Induction Q1 – Q20.
  - Speed Drill: Testing $n=1$ and $n=2$ to match options in 15 seconds.
- **Day 21: Weekly Diagnostic Mock 3**
  - Mock Test 3: 50 randomized questions covering Complex Numbers, Binomial, and Induction.

  - Practice: BSG Matrices and Determinant Q46 – Q60.
  - Speed Drill: Cramer’s rule for $x$ without solving for $y$.
- **Day 25: The 540-Question "Mistake Vault" Review**
  - Review all questions previously answered incorrectly or flagged as tricky.
- **Day 26: Full CBT Simulation 1 (FUTMINNA Standard)**
  - 50 questions, strictly timed (45 minutes). Target: $\ge 42/50$ (84%).
- **Day 27: Full CBT Simulation 2**
  - 50 questions, strictly timed (45 minutes). Target: $\ge 45/50$ (90%).
- **Day 28: High-Yield Speed Hack Drill**
  - Practice 40 random questions with a strict 30-second cap per question using only shortcuts.
- **Day 29: Final Formula Bible & Flashcard Blitz**
  - Complete review of the 2-Page Formula Bible and all 10 Flashcard decks.
- **Day 30: Rest, Mindset & Exam Readiness**
  - Light 20-question confidence booster. Early rest and mental preparation for the exam hall!
